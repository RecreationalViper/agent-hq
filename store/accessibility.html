Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our site and our printables.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Our approach

We build this site to work well on phones, with screen readers and with keyboard navigation, with readable text sizes and clear contrast. Images and videos include text descriptions.

Our files

Our printables are fill-in PDFs that work in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app, which supports screen readers and zoom.

Need help?

If anything on this site or in our files is hard to use, please tell us or email foreverheld.shop@gmail.com. We'll help you get what you need and fix the problem.
