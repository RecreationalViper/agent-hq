Questions families ask

Do I need any special software?

No. The files are fill-in PDFs. Open them in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app on a phone or computer and type over the sample wording.

Can I add a photo?

Yes, on a computer: open the program or sign in Adobe Acrobat Reader and click the circle in the tree to choose a photo. Without one, the circle prints as a soft sage frame.

How quickly can I have it ready?

Right away. You download the files at checkout, and most families finish editing in under an hour.

Will anything be mailed to me?

No. This is a digital download, so there's no shipping to wait for. You print it yourself or at a local print shop.

Can a print shop print the welcome sign?

Yes. Save your filled-in sign and ask for it to be printed at 24 × 36, 16 × 24 or 12 × 18 in and mounted on foam board, usually the same day.
