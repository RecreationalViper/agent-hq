How to Use a Funeral Program Template

A funeral program template gives you a ready‑made layout that you can fill in on your phone or computer, then print at home or a local shop. It provides a comforting way to share the order of service, life story, and thank‑you notes with guests.

What Does a Funeral Program Template Include?

Our Soft Botanical suite provides a four‑panel program that folds to a 5.5 × 8.5 in booklet. The front cover welcomes guests, the inside panels hold the order of service, a short life story, and a thank‑you/reception note. All pages are formatted for standard US Letter paper (11 × 8.5 in) so you can print on any home or office printer.

  • Cover – gentle botanical design with space for a photo and name
  • Order of Service – timeline of the ceremony
  • Life Story – brief biography or favorite memories
  • Thank‑You/Reception – gratitude and reception details

How to Personalize the Template on a Phone or Computer

Open the PDF in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app (available for iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS). Tap any sample text, type your information, and use the built‑in Save function. On a computer you can also add a photo by selecting the image placeholder and choosing a file from your hard drive.

  • Download the PDF
  • Open in Adobe Acrobat Reader
  • Tap or click each text box and type
  • Add a photo (computer only)
  • Save the file

Printing the Program at Home

Print the saved PDF on 80‑100 lb cardstock for a sturdy booklet. Choose “Landscape” orientation, select “Fit to Page,” and print double‑sided if your printer supports it. After printing, fold the sheet in half to create the 5.5 × 8.5 in program.

  • Use US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) paper
  • Select cardstock for durability
  • Print double‑sided, landscape
  • Fold in half to form the booklet

Printing at a Local Shop (Staples, Office Depot, FedEx)

If you prefer a professional finish, bring the saved PDF file on a USB drive or email it to the store. Ask for the program printed on 100 lb cardstock, double‑sided, and cut to the exact size. Many shops can also mount the welcome sign on foam board for a polished entry display.

  • Save PDF to USB or email
  • Request 100 lb cardstock, double‑sided
  • Ask for a clean cut to 5.5 × 8.5 in
  • Mount the welcome sign on foam board (optional)

Questions

Can I edit a funeral program template without paying for software?

Yes. The PDF can be edited with the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app on any phone, tablet, or computer—no subscription or design program needed.

What paper size should I use for the program?

Print on standard US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) paper; after folding, the booklet measures 5.5 × 8.5 in.

Do I need to print the program double‑sided?

Double‑sided printing gives a professional look and saves paper, but you can also print one side, then manually flip and print the back if your printer lacks duplex capability.

Can I add a photo to the program?

Photos can be added on a computer by clicking the image placeholder in Adobe Reader. The mobile app does not support image insertion.

How quickly can I have the program printed?

If you print at home, it’s ready within minutes after saving. At a local print shop, most stores can finish the job the same day.
