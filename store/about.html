About Forever Held

Gentle printable keepsakes for the days families need them most.

Why we exist

When someone you love dies, there are a hundred decisions and very little time. A program, a welcome sign and a place for guests to share memories shouldn't be one more thing to worry about.

Forever Held makes printable memorial pieces that are calm, beautiful and simple to finish: you type your loved one's details on your phone and print them the same day, at home or at a local print shop.

What we believe

  • Simple beats fancy. No design skills, accounts or subscriptions. Just fill-in files that work.
  • Honest previews. What you see on this site is exactly what you download.
  • Real help. If something doesn't work, a real person will help you fix it. See our refund policy.

Our designs

Our first collection is the Soft Botanical suite: sage leaves and cream paper, with a folded program, a welcome sign and share-a-memory cards. More designs are on the way.
