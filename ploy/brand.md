# Forever Held Brand Guidelines (copied from Ploy, 2026-11-12)

## Brand
- Memorial printables for families saying goodbye; gentle, practical support for celebrating a life.

## Colors
- Warm ivory `#f3efe7`: page and printable-paper background. Soft off-white `#faf9f6`: cards and light surfaces.
- Deep botanical green `#3d4a36`: primary text. Muted green-gray `#5d6656`: secondary text.
- Sage `#647358`: primary accent and buttons. Borders `#dad8ce`; subtle borders `#efeeed`.

## Typography
- Headlines: Lora (serif), 400–500. Body/UI: system sans-serif (SF Pro Text, Inter, Segoe UI, Roboto); bold 600; buttons 500.

## Logo
- "Forever Held" wordmark with a small botanical sprig mark (`store/brand/mark.svg`).

## Key components
- Floating pill navigation (wordmark, text links, sage pill button). Hero: large serif headline, short sans copy, filled + outlined pill buttons.
- Product previews in softly rounded frames; warm ivory surfaces, restrained borders, sage accents.

## Voice and copy
- Gentle, reassuring, practical; say "memorial printables". Pair comfort with concrete help: name the printable, how to personalize it on a phone, how/when to print.
- Each item gets its own purpose and format. Steps = action + immediate result.
- Suite imagery is a product preview, not a customer story. No unsupported promises, shipping claims, or unverified prices/destinations.

## Imagery
- Product photography: quiet memorial still life (candlelight, flowers, softly lit window), warm diffused grading, wide crop, rounded frame.
- Printable artwork: refined botanical illustration (leafy branches, wreath/tree motifs, small blossoms) on cream paper; fine linework, open space; sage, olive, gray-green, cream, restrained blush.
