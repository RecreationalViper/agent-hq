## Common Viral Patterns (The Short-Form Formula)

* **Length:** 11–25 seconds for raw clip breakdowns; 30–40 seconds for narrative character edits.
* **Thumbnail/Hook:** Bold text on screen establishing high stakes in 0–2 seconds ("They Thought...", "The Hardest Test...", "Real Stunt...").
* **Topic Focus:** Underestimation payoffs, character flexes/trolling, real-life stunt facts, and high-intensity test scenes.
* **Audio & Atmosphere:** Phonk/slowed remixes for action and character flexes; clear original dialogue for mind games and TV drama.
