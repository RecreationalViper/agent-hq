### Today’s Task
**Create your 1-page "Template Access & Printing Guide" in Canva.**

---

### Why It Matters
When a grieving family buys your digital memorial template, they receive an instant download file. Delivering a warm, simple 1-page PDF with clear instructions and a direct link to their Canva template prevents stress, reduces support messages, and creates a peaceful buying experience. You will use a copy of this single master PDF for every product you sell on Payhip (and later, Etsy).

---

### Exactly What to Do on Your iPhone

1. **Open the Canva app** and tap the **`+`** icon.
2. Select **Document** (or search "US Letter Document", 8.5 x 11 in).
3. Choose a clean, calm background (plain white or soft off-white/cream).
4. Tap **`+` > Text** and add these three short sections (keep fonts simple, such as *Playfair Display* for headers and *Open Sans* or *Cormorant Garamond* for body text):
   * **Warm Thank You:**  
     *"Thank you for letting us support you during this time. We hope this template brings comfort and ease as you honor your loved one."*
   * **Access Button / Link (Placeholder):**  
     Add a rounded rectangle shape with text inside:  
     `[ Click Here to Open Your Template in Canva ]`  
     *(You will paste the actual Canva template link here for each listing later).*
   * **Simple Printing Tips (3 bullet points):**  
     * *Recommended Paper:* Cardstock (80–110 lb / 200–300 gsm) in matte or soft linen finish.  
     * *Where to Print:* Print at home or send the PDF to local print shops (such as Staples, Office Depot, or a local printer) for same-day pickup.  
     * *Need Help?* Add a gentle note with your customer service email.
5. Tap the three dots **`...`** at the top right, tap the title, and rename the file:  
   **`MASTER - Template Access Guide`**.

---

### How to Know It’s Done
* You have a finished, 1-page document saved in your Canva projects.
* It looks clean, uncluttered, and easy to read on a mobile screen.
* No money was spent, and it is ready to duplicate whenever you finalize a specific memorial product.
