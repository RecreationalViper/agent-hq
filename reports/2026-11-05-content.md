Here is a plan for this week’s 5 free social media posts (suitable for TikTok or Pinterest Idea Pins).

### **Idea 1: Simple Setup for a Memory Table**
* **Goal:** Provide helpful, low-stress inspiration for families planning a service.
* **Hook:** "If you’re planning a celebration of life and feeling overwhelmed, here is a simple place to start."
* **Script / Text (20–30s):**  
  "Creating a memory table doesn’t need to be complicated. A simple framed photo, a quiet sign, and cards for guests to write down a favorite story. It gives loved ones a quiet space to pause, reflect, and share memories you can keep forever."
* **On-Screen Visuals:**  
  Slow, peaceful pan over a real printed *Share-a-Memory* card and sign set next to a simple framed photo and a pen on a neutral wooden table. Soft, natural lighting.

---

### **Idea 2: Editing a Program Right from Your Phone**
* **Goal:** Show how quick and gentle the process is using an iPhone.
* **Hook:** "A gentle reminder: you don’t need a computer or design skills to make a memorial program."
* **Script / Text (20–30s):**  
  "When you’re grieving, even opening a laptop can feel like too much. You can edit text, change photos, and adjust details directly on your phone using Canva. Save it as a PDF when you’re ready to print."
* **On-Screen Visuals:**  
  Screen recording or phone video showing a hand gently editing a name and photo on the *Soft Watercolor Sky* program template inside the Canva mobile app.

### **Idea 3: What Goes Inside a Celebration of Life Program?**
* **Goal:** Practical guidance for overwhelmed families.
* **Hook:** "Not sure what to put inside a funeral program? Here is a calm, simple layout."
* **Script / Text (20–30s):**  
  "Cover: A favorite photo, name, and dates.  
  Inside left: The order of service or special music.  
  Inside right: A short biography, poem, or photo collage.  
  Back: A message of thanks from your family."
* **On-Screen Visuals:**  
  Hands gently opening a folded printed sample of the *Classic Elegance* program, pointing softly to each section as the script plays.

---

### **Idea 4: 3 Quick Tips for Last-Minute Printing**
* **Goal:** Solve a common stress point (tight timelines).
* **Hook:** "Three gentle tips for printing memorial templates on a short timeline."
* **Script / Text (20–30s):**  
  "1. Save your completed file as 'PDF Print' for clear text.  
  2. Local print shops like Staples or FedEx can often print same-day or next-day.  
  3. Heavy cardstock (around 80–110 lb) gives programs and signs a sturdy, quality feel."
* **On-Screen Visuals:**  
  Clean aesthetic video showing high-quality printed *Soft Botanical* cards being stacked neatly. On-screen text overlays matching the 3 points.

---

### **Idea 5: Highlighting Minimalist Grace**
* **Goal:** Showcase the *Modern Minimal* style for families looking for clean simplicity.
* **Hook:** "Honoring a loved one with quiet simplicity."
* **Script / Text (20–30s):**  
  "Not every service needs bright colors or heavy decorations. Clean white space and gentle typography let their memory—and their story—stay at the center of the room."
* **On-Screen Visuals:**  
  Close-up shot of a printed *Modern Minimal* Welcome Sign placed on a simple wooden easel with a single stem of eucalyptus.
