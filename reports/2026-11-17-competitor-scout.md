## 3️⃣ Five Competitor Tactics to Consider (Copy, Improve, or Beat)

| # | What competitors do | How we can respond |
|---|----------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Bundle multiple pieces** (program + sign + memory cards) – e.g., SimplePrintables, Graceful Goodbyes. | Keep the **3‑piece bundle** (program, 24×36 in welcome sign, 4.25×5.5 in memory card).  Consider adding a *tiny “Thank‑you” card* as a free extra to out‑bundle them. |
| 2 | **Offer multiple paper sizes** (US Letter, A4, 11×17) – Quill & Star provides 4 size options. | Highlight that our bundle is **US Letter only** (simplifies printing) but add a *note* that users can upscale the sign to 16×24 or 12×18 without loss of quality (already in the product description). |
| 3 | **Provide a “Start Here” guide** – holiday collections include a step‑by‑step PDF. | Add a **one‑page “How to Fill & Print” guide** to every purchase (already part of the holiday bundles). Make it the first page of the PDF so it’s automatically visible. |
| 4 | **Allow online editing with live preview** – Quill & Star’s 30‑day editor, Etsy sellers’ editable PDFs. | Emphasize that our PDFs work **in free Adobe Acrobat Reader** on any device, with *real‑time preview* (the PDF shows the filled‑in fields instantly). Mention the 30‑day “open‑and‑edit” window (we can state that the PDF can be reopened and edited any time). |
| 5 | **Seasonal or themed designs** (Thanksgiving Harvest Table, Christmas Evergreen). | Continue the **seasonal holiday sets** (already planned) and consider a **“Spring Renewal”** set for April‑May to fill the gap between Thanksgiving and Christmas. |
