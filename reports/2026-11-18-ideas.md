# Product Ideas - 2026-11-18

Here are 3 new product ideas tailored to the **Modern Minimal** and **Soft Watercolor Sky** styles (since Soft Botanical and Classic Elegance are already in progress or live). These are designed to capture high-intent search traffic and fit specific emotional or logistical niches within the existing 90-day roadmap.

### 1. The "Scatter & Remembrance" Set (Style: Modern Minimal)
*   **What it is:** A clean, high-contrast set for scattering ashes or releasing remembrances. Includes a simple, elegant **Memorial Card** (to be placed on a table or given to guests) and a **Scattering Certificate** (a formal, minimalist document acknowledging the act of remembrance).
*   **Who buys it and when:** Families planning a private, non-traditional farewell or a "remembrance ceremony" rather than a traditional service. Often bought 2–4 weeks before a private gathering or a specific "first anniversary" date.
*   **Pages/Sizes Included:**
    *   1x Memorial Table Sign: 8x10 in (US Letter landscape).
    *   4x Remembrance Cards: 4.25x5.5 in (2 per US Letter sheet).
    *   1x Scattering Certificate: 8.5x11 in (US Letter portrait) with space for date, location, and names.
*   **Suggested Price:** **$9.99**

### 2. "Sky Memory" Keepsake Bundle (Style: Soft Watercolor Sky)
*   **What it is:** A set centered around the "going up" or "angel" motif, using soft blues and whites. Includes a **Window Cling/Decal** (for a home or grave site) and **Sky-themed Memory Cards** for guests to fill out during a service.
*   **Who buys it and when:** Families in the "adjustment" phase (1–3 months post-loss) who are decorating their home or a specific memorial spot. It’s a "comfort" purchase rather than a "service day" purchase.
*   **Pages/Sizes Included:**
    *   1x Window Cling/Decal: 8.5x11 in (US Letter) with a "In Loving Memory" arc and soft cloud motifs.
    *   4x Sky Memory Cards: 4.25x5.5 in (2 per US Letter sheet).
    *   1x "Letter to the Sky" Journal Page: 8.5x11 in (US Letter) with gentle prompts.
*   **Suggested Price:** **$11.99**

### 3. The "Final Farewell" Service Suite (Style: Classic Elegance)
*   **What it is:** A refined, premium-feeling kit for more formal or large-scale services. It focuses on the "Welcome" and the "Order of Service" with a classic, timeless look (serif fonts, thin borders).
*   **Who buys it and when:** Families with a larger guest list or a more traditional church/funeral home setting. Usually purchased 1–2 weeks before the event. This targets the "Classic Elegance" audience who may feel the "Soft Botanical" is too informal.
*   **Pages/Sizes Included:**
    *   1x Welcome Sign: 24x36 in (2:3 ratio, prints 12x18 or 16x24).
    *   4x Folded Programs: 11x8.5 in (US Letter landscape) folding to 5.5x8.5 in.
    *   4x Memorial Bookmarks: 3x6 in (4 per US Letter sheet).
*   **Suggested Price:** **$14.99**
