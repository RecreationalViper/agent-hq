## One Partnership Idea

**“Family-First Sample Kit”**  
Give the partner a free, branded mini-kit (one folded program, one welcome sign, one share-a-memory card — all watermarked “SAMPLE”) they can hand to families during arrangement meetings. Families see the quality, fill in a few fields on a phone, and if they want the full editable suite they use the partner’s unique discount code (e.g., `PARTNER20` for 20 % off). The partner pays nothing; Forever Held gets warm referrals; families get a tangible, no-pressure preview.
