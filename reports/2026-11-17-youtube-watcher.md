## 🎬 What MotionVault Should Clip

Based on these trends, here are specific directions for Claude (the editor) and the owner:

### 1. The "Underestimated" Arc (Movie/Anime)
*   **Concept:** Find a scene where a character looks weak/ignored but then delivers a powerful comeback or reveal.
*   **Source Ideas:** *The Dark Knight* (Joker intro), *Demon Slayer* (Tanjiro vs. lower demons), *Avengers* (Hawkeye/Clint Barton moments).
*   **Title Pattern:** `"They Underestimated the [Role]" 🗿🔥`
*   **Edit:** 2s of them being mocked/ignored → 5s of the buildup → 3s of the payoff. End on a freeze-frame or strong reaction.

### 2. The "Confused/Clueless" Reaction (Game/Movie)
*   **Concept:** A character (or player) fails to understand basic mechanics or a situation.
*   **Source Ideas:** *Portal* (Chell's first reaction), *Minecraft* (Steve staring at a wall), *Dumb and Dumber* (Stu's reactions).
*   **Title Pattern:** `The Most Confused [Character/Role] Ever 🤣😭`
*   **Edit:** Loop the confused expression. Use a sound effect (record scratch, "bruh") to enhance the comedy. Keep it under 15s.

### 3. The "I Just Wanted To..." Relatable Meme (Movie)
*   **Concept:** A character has a simple goal, but it goes wrong immediately.
*   **Source Ideas:** *Superman III* (Superman trying to be human), *Home Alone* (Kevin's plans), *The Office* (Michael Scott's attempts).
*   **Title Pattern:** `I just wanted to [simple action]`
*   **Edit:** Show the intent (2s) → Show the disaster (5s) → Cut to black or reaction (2s). No music, just natural audio + one comedic sound effect.

### 4. The "Mid-Game/Scene" Interruption (Game)
*   **Concept:** A serious moment interrupted by something absurd.
*   **Source Ideas:** *GTA V* (random NPC events), *Red Dead Redemption 2* (strange wildlife), *Elden Ring* (unexpected enemy spawns).
*   **Title Pattern:** `When [Absurd Thing] Happens Mid-Game! 😂`
*   **Edit:** 3s of tense gameplay → 5s of the absurd interruption → 2s of player reaction (if visible) or character freeze.
