### 3️⃣ Recurring Series Idea – **“What‑If X Meets Y?”**

**Concept** – Each Saturday we mash‑up two *unrelated* IPs that are both trending that week (game‑movie, anime‑game, etc.).  
- **Structure (30‑35 s):**  
  1. **Hook (0‑2 s):** Flash of both characters side‑by‑side, bold text “What‑If X Meets Y?”  
  2. **Story Beat 1 (2‑12 s):** Quick cut of Character A in an iconic action moment.  
  3. **Story Beat 2 (12‑22 s):** Same‑style moment from Character B, matched to the same beat/audio.  
  4. **Blend (22‑30 s):** Split‑screen or quick transition showing a *crossover* visual (e.g., Lucia riding a Spider‑Man web line).  
  5. **Final Beat (30‑35 s):** Freeze‑frame with a question “Who would win?” and black cut.  

**Why it works:**  
- Leverages *cross‑audience curiosity* (fans of each IP click).  
- Easy to produce from existing footage (≤ 5 s per source).  
- Generates comments/duels (“Team Lucia vs. Team Spidey”).  
- Can be repurposed as a carousel on TikTok/YouTube Shorts.

**First three episodes (pre‑planned):**

### 6️⃣ Quick Production Checklist (for Claude)

1. **Vertical format** – 1080 × 1920, safe‑zone text (keep important info > 100 px from edges).  
2. **Hook ≤ 2 s** – loud, on‑beat audio + bold text overlay.  
3. **Unbroken stretches** – keep each source clip ≤ 6 s; avoid long copyrighted runs.  
4. **Audio sync** – place major visual beat on the audio drop (click, bass hit, vocal cue).  
5. **Credits** – small lower‑third title with source name (e.g., “GTA VI Trailer”, “Overgeared EP 9”).  
6. **Final beat** – cut to black *right before* the climax impact to force re‑watch.  
7. **File naming** – `YYYYMMDD_<IP>_Hook_<AudioTag>.mp4` for easy tracking.
