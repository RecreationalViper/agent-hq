# Daily Focus - 2026-11-10

## ✅ Today’s 30‑Minute Task  
**Create a simple, phone‑friendly product image for the *Soft Botanical* bundle and add it to the Payhip product page.**

### Why it matters
- **First impressions count:** A clear, attractive image makes the listing look professional and helps grieving families feel confident that they’re buying a quality, ready‑to‑use PDF.
- **Boosts click‑through:** Payhip (and later Etsy) show the first image in search results and social shares. A good thumbnail can increase traffic and, eventually, sales.
- **No cost, no extra tools:** You can do it entirely on your iPhone using the free **Canva** app (the free version is enough for a basic mock‑up).

### Step‑by‑step (iPhone)

1. **Gather the PDF previews**  
   - Open the four fill‑in PDFs on your iPhone (you should have them saved from the design folder).  
   - Take a screenshot of the *first page* of each PDF (press **Side button + Volume Up**).  
   - You’ll have 4 screenshots; these show the program layout, welcome sign, and memory card.

2. **Open Canva (free app)**  
   - Tap **Create a design → Custom size**. Set size to **1200 × 1800 px** (portrait). This works well for Payhip thumbnails and later Etsy listings.

3. **Build the mock‑up**  
   - Add a **white background**.  
   - Drag the 4 screenshots onto the canvas. Arrange them in a 2 × 2 grid, leaving a small gap between each.  
   - Add a **text box** at the top: “Soft Botanical Memorial Kit” (choose a calm serif or simple sans‑serif font, 36 pt, dark green or charcoal).  
   - Add a subtitle below: “Fill‑in PDFs – Program, Welcome Sign, Memory Card” (28 pt, lighter gray).  
   - Keep it simple—no extra graphics, just the product itself.

4. **Export**  
   - Tap the **download** arrow → choose **PNG** → “Download”. The file will save to your Photos.

5. **Upload to Payhip**  
   - Open the Payhip app or Safari → log in.  
   - Go to **Products → Soft Botanical Bundle → Edit**.  
   - Tap the current image placeholder → **Replace** → select the PNG you just saved.  
   - Click **Save**.

6. **Confirm it’s live**  
   - Open the public product link: **https://payhip.com/b/vqNSA**.  
   - Verify that the new image appears at the top of the page (refresh if needed).  

### Done when:
- You have a clean, 2 × 2 screenshot collage saved as a PNG on your iPhone.  
- That PNG is uploaded as the main image on the Payhip product page.  
- You can see the updated image when you view the public link.
