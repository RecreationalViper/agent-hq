# Product Ideas - 2026-11-25

## 3 New Memorial Printable Product Ideas

All three fit the four existing styles (Classic Elegance, Modern Minimal, Soft Watercolor Sky, Soft Botanical) and use the same fill-in PDF workflow (free Adobe Acrobat Reader, no Canva). Each needs owner approval before building.

---

### 1. Memorial Guest Book Pages
**What it is** — A set of prompted pages guests sign at the service. Families print as many copies as needed, then keep the filled pages in a binder or have them coil-bound.

**Who buys & when** — Immediate family planning a service (1–3 weeks before). Also funeral directors buying on behalf of families.

**Pages / sizes (US Letter only)**
- Cover page — 8.5×11 in (name, dates, photo frame, “In Loving Memory”)
- Signature spread — 8.5×11 in, 2-up layout (left: lined “Name & Memory”; right: lined “Name & Memory”) — print double-sided
- Photo memory page — 8.5×11 in, 4 photo frames + caption lines
- Blank lined page — 8.5×11 in (extra writing space)
- Start Here guide (print tips, binding options)

**Suggested price** — $7.99 (stand-alone) or $16.99 bundled with any program suite

---

### 2. Memorial Bookmark Set
**What it is** — Keepsake bookmarks attendees take home. Four per US Letter sheet, cut to 2×6 in. Space for photo, name, dates, and a short verse or quote.

**Who buys & when** — Families ordering 1–2 weeks before the service (often alongside the program). Churches/hospices ordering in bulk for recurring services.

**Pages / sizes (US Letter only)**
- Bookmark sheet — 8.5×11 in, 4-up (2×6 in each, cut lines included)
  - Front: photo frame, name, birth/death dates, “In Loving Memory”
  - Back: 4–5 lines for a verse/quote/prayer (fill-in)
- Ink-saver version (light background)
- Start Here guide (paper weight, cutting, laminating tips)

**Suggested price** — $5.99

---

### 3. Memorial Thank-You Card Set
**What it is** — Folded thank-you cards families send after the service. Matches the share-a-memory card size (4.25×5.5 in folded) so envelopes are easy to source.

**Who buys & when** — Families 1–4 weeks after the service. Also grief supporters buying as a gift for the bereaved.

**Pages / sizes (US Letter only)**
- Card sheet — 8.5×11 in, 2-up (each folds to 4.25×5.5 in)
  - Front: photo frame, name, dates, “Thank You”
  - Inside: fill-in message area (3–4 lines) + signature line
- Envelope template — 8.5×11 in, prints A2 envelope (4.375×5.75 in) — optional liner
- Ink-saver version
- Start Here guide (printing, folding, envelope assembly)

**Suggested price** — $6.99
