## 3. Five Things Competitors Do That We Should Copy or Beat

| # | Competitor Practice | Why It Matters | Our Move (draft) |
|---|---------------------|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | **“Try before you buy” free demo** (EdiDesignCollection, IoncreativaStudio) | Lowers hesitation; grieving buyers want to know it works *before* paying. | Build a **live demo** of one fill-in field (e.g., name/date) on our storefront; link from listing. |
| 2 | **Clear “what goes on each page” table** (AmourPrint) | Answers the #1 question: “What do I actually write where?” | Add a **simple page-by-page table** to every listing (program, sign, card). |
| 3 | **Multiple sizes included** (Quill & Star: 4 sizes; Ioncreativa: 3 sizes) | Families print at home (Letter/A4) or at a shop (11×17, tabloid). | Our program is **US Letter only** — consider adding **11×17** and **A4** PDFs to the bundle. |
| 4 | **Matching add-ons shown in-listing** (EdiDesignCollection cross-sells prayer card, bookmark, thank-you card) | Increases AOV; families want a cohesive suite. | Bundle **bookmark** and **thank-you card** PDFs into the next collection; show them in listing images. |
| 5 | **Authority signal: licensed funeral director** (Serene Farewells) | Builds instant trust in a high-stakes purchase. | Add a **“Designed with funeral-director input”** line (if true) or a **“Reviewed by a celebrant”** note. |
