## Description

**Evergreen & Candlelight** — a gentle Christmas memorial suite for families spending the holidays without someone they love. Designed to be filled in on any device (computer, tablet, or phone) using the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app — no Canva, no paid software, no design skills needed.

### What you get (4 fill-in PDFs + a Start Here guide)
- **Table Sign — 8×10 in**  
  “This candle burns in loving memory of [Name]” — prints on US Letter; includes a light ink-saver version.
- **Holiday Memory Cards — 4.25×5.5 in, 4 per US Letter sheet**  
  Write a favorite memory, a message, or a prayer. Dark green design + light ink-saver version included.
- **Ornament Keepsake Cards — 3.125×4.375 in arch, 4 per US Letter sheet**  
  Cut out, punch the hole, add ribbon. Hang on the tree or give to family. Dark green + light ink-saver version.
- **Service Program — 11×8.5 in (US Letter landscape), folds to 5.5×8.5 in, 4 panels**  
  Order of service, names remembered, candle-lighting words. Prints on US Letter (no A4).
- **Start Here Guide** — one-page PDF with simple steps: open → type → print → cut/fold.

### How it works
1. Purchase → instant download from Etsy (or Payhip at `https://payhip.com/b/rdhVQ`).
2. Open each PDF in the free **Adobe Acrobat Reader** (desktop, iOS, or Android).
3. Click the highlighted fields, type your loved one’s name, dates, memories, service details.
4. Print at home on US Letter paper or send to a local print shop.
5. Cut/fold where indicated. Done.

### Print specs
- All files sized for **US Letter (8.5×11 in)** — no A4 versions.
- Colors: pine green, cream, brass, cranberry; hand-drawn pine, berries, one candle.
- Fonts: GFS Baskerville (headlines) + Inter (UI); fill-in text renders in Times/Helvetica on any device.

### A note on color
Home printers vary. The light ink-saver versions use less ink and work well on standard printers. For deepest color, a local print shop on matte cardstock gives beautiful results.
