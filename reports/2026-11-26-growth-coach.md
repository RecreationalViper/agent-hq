## 3️⃣ One risk to fix **right now**  

### ⚠️ Copyright reuse of long trailer footage  

- The GTA VI edit (and the other movie/game clips) currently uses **~15 seconds of a single trailer segment**.  
- TikTok and YouTube’s automated filters often flag any *continuous* clip longer than **≈3 seconds** unless it’s transformed (adds commentary, visual effects, overlays, or is used under “fair use” style).  
- **Immediate mitigation:**  
  1. **Add on‑screen text commentary** (e.g., “Detail #1: the neon logo is a nod to 1998’s Vice City”) throughout the skyline portion.  
  2. **Apply a subtle visual effect** (zoom‑pan, color‑grade shift, glitch overlay) to make the clip *transformative*.  
  3. **Keep each original source stretch ≤ 3 seconds** where possible – break the skyline reveal into two 2‑second cuts separated by a quick text flash.  

Doing this now reduces the chance of a strike or a “video removed for copyright” that would kill momentum.
