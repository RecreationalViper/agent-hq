### 2. Price Positioning
*   **Market Range:** Most DIY templates sit between **$2.90 and $7.98**.
*   **Premium/Custom Range:** Customized services or high-page-count collages go up to **$16.57+**.
*   **Our Position ($12.99):** We are in the **"Premium DIY"** tier.
    *   *Risk:* Buyers may hesitate at $12.99 vs. $7.49 competitors.
    *   *Mitigation:* We must emphasize that we **do not require a Canva Pro account** (unlike many $10+ Canva templates that often push features requiring paid Canva) and that we include **3 items** (Program + Sign + Card) vs. competitors often selling just the program.
    *   *Action:* Ensure our listing clearly states "No Paid Software Needed" to justify the price over $5-$7 Canva templates.

### 3. 5 Things to Copy or Beat
1.  **"One Purchase, One Person" Clarity:** Quill and Star [2] explicitly states "One purchase one person's program." This reduces support tickets. *Action:* Add this line to our description to set expectations.
2.  **30-Day Editing Window:** Quill and Star [2] allows 30 days to open/edit. *Action:* Since we are a PDF, we can't control this, but we can add a "Troubleshooting" note: "If you have trouble opening, email us within 30 days and we will help."
3.  **Curated Readings/Poems:** DownloadSuite [1] includes a "library of service readings." *Action:* Consider adding a small "Suggested Poems & Verses" text file to our bundle download. It adds value with zero extra cost.
4.  **Size Variety for Signs:** Fig & Laurel [9] offers 18x24 and 24x36. We offer 24x36 (and 16x24/12x18). *Action:* Make sure our listing explicitly lists **all three** sign sizes so buyers know it fits their local print shop.
5.  **Free Simple Options as Lead Magnet:** FuneralFolio [5] offers free basic programs. *Action:* We cannot give away our Soft Botanical design, but we could create a *very* simple "Thank You" card or "In Loving Memory" bookmark as a free download to capture emails (if we ever set up an email list) or to drive traffic. *Note: This requires design time.*

### 4. 3 Ideas for Next Product
1.  **"The After Service Kit":** Competitors sell programs and signs. Few sell items for *after* the service.
    *   *Product:* Enclosed "Thank You" cards for guests who send flowers/calls, plus a simple "Memory Jar" prompt card (small cards to write memories on to put in a jar).
    *   *Gap:* DownloadSuite [1] has thank-you cards, but not paired with a jar concept.
2.  **Photo Collage Program (8-Page):** Loving Memory Prints [7] sells an 8-page photo collage program for $16.57.
    *   *Gap:* Our current program is 4 panels. An 8-page "Booklet" version of our Soft Botanical style would allow for more photos, a higher price point ($15-$18), and appeal to families who want to include more images.
3.  **Matching "Order of Service" Insert for Churches:**
    *   *Gap:* Many services happen in churches with existing hymnals. A simple, single-page "Special Order of Service" insert (half-page size) that matches our Soft Botanical colors would be a helpful add-on.

### 5. Wording Buyers Respond To
