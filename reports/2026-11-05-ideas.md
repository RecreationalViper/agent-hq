# Product Ideas - 2026-11-05

Here are 3 new memorial printable product ideas designed to match our 4 core styles (*Classic Elegance, Modern Minimal, Soft Watercolor Sky, Soft Botanical*). 

All products are created as editable Canva templates with ready-to-print PDF options, designed for easy editing on mobile or desktop.

---

### 1. Memorial Keepsake Bookmarks & Favor Tags

* **What it is:** Double-sided printable memorial bookmarks featuring a loved one's photo, custom dates, and a poem or Bible verse, along with matching small favor/ribbon tags.
* **Who buys it & when:** Family members or funeral planners 3–7 days before a service. They want a long-lasting, portable keepsake for attendees to place in Bibles or books.
* **Pages & sizes included:**
  * Double-sided Bookmark template: 2" x 6" (formatted 4 per US Letter / A4 sheet for easy home printing).
  * Favor / Ribbon Tag template: 2" x 3.5" (formatted 8 per sheet).
* **Suggested price:** $5.99

---

### 2. Celebration of Life Photo Collage Poster

* **What it is:** A structured photo display board template holding 10–15 photos, custom memorial dates, and a central tribute quote or name placement for display at the venue entrance or memory table.
* **Who buys it & when:** Adult children or spouses 4–10 days before a celebration of life service who need a clean, meaningful focal point without manual scrapbooking.
* **Pages & sizes included:**
  * Canva poster templates in 3 standard print sizes: 16" x 20", 18" x 24", and 24" x 36".
* **Suggested price:** $8.99

---

### 3. Bereavement Thank You & Acknowledgement Cards

* **What it is:** Folded note cards designed to match our main program kits, giving families a graceful way to express gratitude for flowers, meals, donations, and support.
* **Who buys it & when:** Primary next-of-kin 1–3 weeks *after* the service, when they are ready to send personal thank-you notes to attendees and donors.
* **Pages & sizes included:**
  * Folded card template: 4" x 6" folded (8" x 6" flat) — fits standard A6 envelopes.
  * Folded card template: 3.5" x 5" folded (7" x 5" flat) — fits standard A2 envelopes.
  * Both sizes provided as single card templates and 2-per-page print layouts.
* **Suggested price:** $6.99
