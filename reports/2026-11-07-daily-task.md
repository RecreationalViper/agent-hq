# Daily Focus - 2026-11-07

### Today’s Focus: Add a Gentle “How It Works” Section to One Listing

**Estimated Time:** 15–20 minutes  
**Area:** Listing Optimization  

---

### Why It Matters
Families planning a memorial service are often emotionally exhausted and operating under tight deadlines. If they cannot tell within five seconds how easy a template is to edit and print, they will click away. Adding a brief, reassuring three-step guide to your listing description removes anxiety and helps them buy with confidence.

---

### Exactly What to Do (on your iPhone)

1. **Pick one listing** in your Etsy Seller app (recommendation: your *Soft Botanical* or *Classic Elegance* Celebration of Life Program).
2. **Open listing edit mode** and scroll to the description box.
3. **Copy and paste the draft below** near the very top of your description (just beneath your opening summary sentence). Adjust details if needed.
4. **Save your listing changes.**

#### Draft to Review & Copy:
*(Requires your approval before publishing)*

> **Simple 3-Step Process for Grieving Families:**
> 1. **Instant Access:** Purchase the listing, and Etsy immediately provides a PDF link to open your template in free Canva (no paid subscription needed).
> 2. **Personalize Gently:** Easily change photos, names, dates, and order of service text on a computer or your phone.
> 3. **Print Today:** Download as a print-ready PDF to print at home, send to a local print shop (Staples, Office Depot, FedEx), or use an online printer.
> 
> *Need help? Please send a message—I am here to support you.*

---

### How to Know It’s Done
* The draft has been reviewed, approved, and pasted into the listing description.
* When viewing the listing preview on your phone, the 3 steps are easy to read without having to scroll through walls of text.

*(Note: Platform layouts in the Etsy Seller app may change over time, but listing descriptions can always be edited via the Listings tab.)*
