  * **Our Key Competitive Edge:** Most competitors forcing buyers into Canva require a Canva account or web browser editing. Our fill-in PDF format (edited in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app) is far simpler for grieving families on mobile devices who do not want to register for design platforms.

### 3. 5 Competitor Tactics We Should Copy or Beat

1. **Include Sample Wording / Pre-written Scriptures:** Competitors like *DownloadSuite* [2] include pre-written poems, scriptures, and eulogy outlines. Stressed families often struggle with blank fields; pre-populating our fill-in PDFs with gentle placeholder wording will make editing much easier.
2. **Step-by-Step Mobile Editing PDF Guide:** Competitors like *SRsentiments* [5] highlight step-by-step help files. Adding a 1-page "How to edit on iPhone with free Adobe Reader" visual sheet to our download ZIP will reduce buyer friction.
3. **Clear Printing Instructions for Signs:** *Dearest Memorials* [11] explicitly explains how to take PDF files to local print shops (Staples, Walgreens, Office Depot) or online printers for foam-core mounting. Adding simple print guidance for our 24x36 sign will reassure buyers.
4. **Offer Matching Digital Announcement / Text Graphics:** Competitors like *FuneralTemplate.com* [22] supply smartphone/WhatsApp invitation graphics. Adding a digital announcement image option captures families holding virtual or hybrid services.
5. **No-Software / Simple Fill-in Highlight:** Competitors emphasizing "No software to download" [2], [10], [13] build buyer confidence. We should clearly market our PDFs as *“No Canva needed — fill in quickly using the free Adobe Reader app on your phone or computer.”*
